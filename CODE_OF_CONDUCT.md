# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that creates a positive environment:

* being kind and patient with people who are new to Kubernetes or to Bash;
* giving and accepting constructive feedback;
* focusing on what is best for the people who run clusters with this tool;
* showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

* insults, personal or political attacks, and dismissive comments;
* public or private harassment, including unwanted sexual attention;
* publishing other people's private information without permission;
* publishing cluster data (host names, namespaces, IPs, credentials) taken from
  an environment you are not allowed to share;
* other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer,
[@dheer629](https://github.com/dheer629), through GitHub. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of
the reporter will be respected.

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block
accounts for behaviour that violates this code of conduct, and will explain the
reason when it is appropriate to do so.
