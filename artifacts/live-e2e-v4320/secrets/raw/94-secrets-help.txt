                path on the node, and what happens after a rotation: volumes are synced by the
                kubelet, env and subPath consumers need a restart.
   THIS HOST    on a node, kdash compares the kubelet's file with the Secret: in sync / STALE.
   FINDINGS     expired / expiring, a Secret that does not exist but is needed, tls.key missing,

                several Secrets. Enter runs the read commands of a finding.
   No access    without the right to read Secrets the references from pods and ingresses are
                still mapped, and the certificate table says "not visible".
