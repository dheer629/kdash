 SECRETS MAP - certificates and keys in Secrets (read-only, only what your RBAC allows).
   Never read   private keys, passwords, tokens: kubectl's template drops every value that is
                not a PEM certificate before kdash receives it. Key names and sizes only.
   CERTIFICATES one row per certificate: state, days left, expiry, subject, SAN, issuer, key
                algorithm, whether its private key is beside it, who manages it, who uses it.
   WHERE USED   workload, the Services in front of it, how it is consumed (volume, projected,
                env, envFrom, imagePull, ingress TLS), the path in the container, the kubelet
                path on the node, and what happens after a rotation: volumes are synced by the
                kubelet, env and subPath consumers need a restart.
   THIS HOST    on a node, kdash compares the kubelet's file with the Secret: in sync / STALE.
   FINDINGS     expired / expiring, a Secret that does not exist but is needed, tls.key missing,
                weak keys, pods still running the previous certificate, the same certificate in
                several Secrets. Enter runs the read commands of a finding.
   No access    without the right to read Secrets the references from pods and ingresses are
                still mapped, and the certificate table says "not visible".
   CLI          kdash --secrets [-o csv|tsv|json|html] [--fail-on high|medium] [--run N]
                KDASH_SECRETS_KEYCHECK=1 also checks that tls.key belongs to tls.crt.
